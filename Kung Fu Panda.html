✦ PO'S FAVOURITE
RECIPE • 01

🥟 Dumplings

Soft and delicious dumplings filled with vegetables and aromatic Asian flavours.

⏱ 35 MIN ✦ MEDIUM
✦

🥟 Dumplings

✦ Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup finely chopped cabbage
  • ¼ cup grated carrot
  • 2 tbsp chopped spring onion
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp ginger
  • Salt and pepper
  • Water as needed

✦ Preparation

  1. Mix flour with water and make a soft dough.
  2. Rest the dough for 15 minutes.
  3. Mix vegetables with soy sauce, ginger and seasoning.
  4. Roll small dough circles.
  5. Add filling and fold the edges.
  6. Steam the dumplings for 10–12 minutes.
  7. Serve warm with dipping sauce.

✦ WARRIOR'S FAVOURITE
RECIPE • 02

🍜 Noodles

Warm and flavourful noodles tossed with colourful vegetables and a delicious Asian sauce.

⏱ 25 MIN ✦ EASY
✧

🍜 Noodles

✦ Ingredients

  • 200 g noodles
  • ½ cup sliced cabbage
  • ½ cup carrot
  • ¼ cup capsicum
  • 2 tbsp spring onion
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

✦ Preparation

  1. Cook noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Drain and keep aside.
  3. Heat sesame oil in a pan.
  4. Add vegetables and stir-fry for a few minutes.
  5. Add soy sauce and chilli sauce.
  6. Add cooked noodles and toss well.
  7. Garnish with spring onion and serve hot.
