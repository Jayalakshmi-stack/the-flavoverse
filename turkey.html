✦ THE TURKISH TABLE ✦

A Taste of Turkey

Turkish cuisine brings together flavours from Europe and Asia. Its culinary traditions feature grilled meats, fresh vegetables, fragrant spices, flatbreads and beautifully layered desserts.

✦ CULINARY JOURNEY ✦

🍽️ Three Turkish Classics

Follow every recipe from ingredients to preparation

01

🥙 Döner Kebab

SAVOURY • AROMATIC • CLASSIC

SAVOURY • JUICY • FRAGRANT

🌿 Ingredients

Chicken Yogurt Garlic Paprika Cumin Black Pepper Lemon Flatbread Tomato Lettuce

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Prepare the Marinade

Mix yogurt with garlic, paprika, cumin, black pepper and lemon juice.

02

Marinate the Chicken

Coat the chicken thoroughly with the marinade and allow the flavours to develop.

03

Cook the Chicken

Cook the marinated chicken in a hot pan or grill until completely cooked and lightly browned.

04

Slice the Chicken

Allow the cooked chicken to rest briefly and slice it into thin pieces.

05

Prepare the Bread

Warm the flatbread lightly before assembling the kebab.

06

Add Fresh Vegetables

Arrange lettuce and tomato over the warm flatbread.

07

Add the Döner

Place the sliced chicken over the vegetables.

08

Serve

Fold the bread and serve the Döner Kebab while warm.

⏱ 45 MINUTES 🍽 4 SERVINGS 🥙 MAIN DISH 🇹🇷 TURKEY
✦
02

🍯 Baklava

SWEET • CRISPY • NUTTY

CRISPY • SWEET • GOLDEN

🌿 Ingredients

Phyllo Pastry Pistachios Walnuts Butter Sugar Water Lemon Juice

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Prepare the Syrup

Simmer sugar, water and lemon juice until a light syrup forms. Allow it to cool.

02

Prepare the Nuts

Finely chop pistachios and walnuts for the filling.

03

Layer the Pastry

Place sheets of phyllo pastry in a baking dish, brushing each layer lightly with butter.

04

Add the Nut Filling

Spread the chopped nuts evenly between layers of pastry.

05

Finish the Layers

Continue layering the pastry and nuts, finishing with several buttered pastry sheets.

06

Cut the Baklava

Cut the layered pastry into neat diamond or square pieces before baking.

07

Bake Until Golden

Bake until the pastry becomes crisp and beautifully golden.

08

Add the Syrup

Pour the cooled syrup over the hot baklava and allow it to absorb.

⏱ 60 MINUTES 🍽 12 PIECES 🍯 DESSERT 🇹🇷 TURKEY
✦
03

🫓 Lahmacun

THIN • CRISPY • SAVOURY

CRISPY • SPICED • AROMATIC

🌿 Ingredients

Flour Minced Meat Onion Tomato Garlic Parsley Paprika Cumin Olive Oil Lemon

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Prepare the Dough

Mix flour with water, yeast and salt to form a soft dough.

02

Rest the Dough

Cover the dough and allow it to rest until it becomes soft and easy to roll.

03

Prepare the Topping

Mix minced meat with finely chopped tomato, onion, garlic and parsley.

04

Season the Mixture

Add paprika, cumin, salt and olive oil and mix everything thoroughly.

05

Roll the Dough

Divide the dough and roll each piece into a very thin round.

06

Spread the Topping

Spread a thin, even layer of the seasoned meat mixture over the dough.

07

Bake

Bake in a very hot oven until the edges become crisp and the topping is cooked.

08

Serve

Add fresh lemon juice and parsley, then serve the Lahmacun warm.

⏱ 40 MINUTES 🍽 4 SERVINGS 🫓 MAIN DISH 🇹🇷 TURKEY
