✦ THE VIETNAMESE TABLE ✦

A Taste of Vietnam

Vietnamese cuisine is celebrated for its balance of fresh herbs, vegetables, noodles, savoury flavours and aromatic ingredients. From comforting bowls of Pho to crispy Banh Mi and refreshing Goi Cuon, every dish tells a story of Vietnamese culinary heritage.

✦ CULINARY JOURNEY ✦

🍽️ Three Vietnamese Classics

Follow every recipe from ingredients to preparation

01

🍜 Pho

FRAGRANT • COMFORTING • CLASSIC

FRAGRANT • WARM • AROMATIC

🌿 Ingredients

Rice Noodles Beef or Chicken Onion Ginger Star Anise Cinnamon Fish Sauce Bean Sprouts Fresh Herbs Lime

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Prepare the Broth

Simmer the meat with onion and ginger to create a flavourful broth.

02

Add the Aromatics

Add star anise, cinnamon and other aromatic spices to the simmering broth.

03

Season the Broth

Add fish sauce and adjust the seasoning to create a balanced savoury broth.

04

Prepare the Noodles

Cook the rice noodles according to the package instructions and drain well.

05

Prepare the Toppings

Arrange bean sprouts, fresh herbs, lime and sliced vegetables for serving.

06

Assemble the Bowl

Place the cooked noodles into a bowl and add sliced cooked meat.

07

Add the Broth

Pour the hot aromatic broth over the noodles and meat.

08

Serve

Garnish with fresh herbs, bean sprouts and lime before serving warm.

⏱ 60 MINUTES🍽 4 SERVINGS🍜 MAIN DISH🇻🇳 VIETNAM
✦
02

🥖 Banh Mi

CRISPY • FRESH • SAVOURY

CRISPY • FRESH • FLAVOURFUL

🌿 Ingredients

Baguette Chicken or Pork Carrot Cucumber Pickled Radish Fresh Coriander Mayonnaise Soy Sauce Chilli

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Prepare the Filling

Cook the chicken or pork with simple Vietnamese-inspired seasonings.

02

Prepare the Pickles

Combine thinly sliced carrot and radish with a light vinegar mixture.

03

Slice the Vegetables

Cut cucumber and chilli into thin, easy-to-eat pieces.

04

Prepare the Baguette

Slice the baguette lengthwise and warm it lightly until crisp outside.

05

Add the Sauce

Spread mayonnaise or your preferred sauce inside the warm baguette.

06

Add the Filling

Place the cooked meat evenly inside the baguette.

07

Add Fresh Toppings

Add cucumber, pickled vegetables, coriander and chilli.

08

Serve

Serve the Banh Mi immediately while the baguette remains crisp and fresh.

⏱ 30 MINUTES🍽 4 SERVINGS🥖 STREET FOOD🇻🇳 VIETNAM
✦
03

🥬 Goi Cuon

FRESH • LIGHT • REFRESHING

FRESH • LIGHT • REFRESHING

🌿 Ingredients

Rice Paper Cooked Shrimp Rice Noodles Lettuce Carrot Cucumber Fresh Mint Coriander Hoisin Sauce

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Prepare the Noodles

Cook the rice noodles according to the package instructions and cool them.

02

Prepare the Vegetables

Slice carrot and cucumber into thin strips and prepare the lettuce.

03

Prepare the Herbs

Wash and prepare fresh mint and coriander for the rolls.

04

Soften the Rice Paper

Dip each rice paper sheet briefly in warm water until it becomes flexible.

05

Add the Lettuce

Place lettuce near the lower centre of the softened rice paper.

06

Add Noodles and Vegetables

Add rice noodles, carrot, cucumber, mint and coriander.

07

Add the Shrimp

Arrange cooked shrimp neatly over the filling.

08

Roll and Serve

Fold the sides inward and roll tightly. Serve fresh with a dipping sauce.

⏱ 30 MINUTES🍽 4 SERVINGS🥬 FRESH ROLLS🇻🇳 VIETNAM
