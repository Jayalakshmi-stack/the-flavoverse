✦ THE INDIAN TABLE ✦

A Feast of Tradition

Indian cuisine is a beautiful collection of regional traditions, aromatic spices and generations-old recipes. From fragrant biryani to creamy butter chicken and delicate gulab jamun, every dish carries its own story.

✦ CULINARY JOURNEY ✦

🍽️ Three Indian Classics

Follow every recipe from ingredients to preparation

01

🍛 Biryani

THE CROWN JEWEL OF INDIAN CUISINE

FRAGRANT • SPICED • TRADITIONAL

🌿 Ingredients

Basmati Rice Chicken Onion Tomato Yogurt Ginger-Garlic Biryani Masala Mint Coriander Saffron

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Prepare the Rice

Wash and soak basmati rice. Cook it in salted water with whole spices until partially cooked.

02

Marinate the Chicken

Mix chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli, turmeric and biryani spices.

03

Prepare the Masala

Cook sliced onions until golden. Add tomatoes, marinated chicken and spices. Cook until tender.

04

Create the Layers

Place partially cooked rice over the chicken masala. Add fried onions, mint, coriander and saffron.

05

Dum Cooking

Cover the pot tightly and cook gently until the rice is cooked and absorbs the flavours.

06

Rest the Biryani

Turn off the heat and allow the biryani to rest briefly.

07

Gently Mix

Fluff the rice gently from the sides without breaking the grains.

08

Serve

Serve hot with raita, boiled egg or a side dish of your choice.

⏱ 1 HOUR 🍽 4 SERVINGS 🍛 MAIN DISH 🇮🇳 INDIA
✦
02

🍗 Butter Chicken

CREAMY • RICH • AROMATIC

CREAMY • SPICED • COMFORTING

🌿 Ingredients

Chicken Yogurt Butter Tomato Cream Ginger-Garlic Garam Masala Chilli Powder Kasuri Methi

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Marinate the Chicken

Combine chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste and spices. Allow the flavours to blend.

02

Cook the Chicken

Cook the marinated chicken until properly cooked and lightly browned.

03

Make the Tomato Base

Cook tomatoes with ginger, garlic and spices until soft.

04

Blend the Sauce

Blend the cooked tomato mixture into a smooth sauce.

05

Add Butter

Warm butter in a pan and add the prepared tomato sauce.

06

Add Chicken

Add the cooked chicken and allow it to simmer gently in the sauce.

07

Finish the Gravy

Add cream and crushed kasuri methi. Stir until the flavours combine.

08

Serve

Serve warm with naan, roti or rice.

⏱ 50 MINUTES 🍽 4 SERVINGS 🍗 MAIN DISH 🇮🇳 INDIA
✦
03

🍬 Gulab Jamun

A CLASSIC INDIAN SWEET

SWEET • SOFT • FESTIVE

🌿 Ingredients

Milk Powder Flour Milk Ghee Sugar Cardamom Rose Water Saffron

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Prepare the Dough

Mix milk powder and flour. Add a little milk and ghee to form a soft dough.

02

Shape the Balls

Divide the dough into small portions and roll them into smooth balls.

03

Prepare the Syrup

Simmer sugar and water to make a light syrup. Add cardamom and rose water.

04

Heat the Ghee

Warm the ghee or suitable cooking oil over moderate heat.

05

Fry the Jamun

Carefully fry the dough balls until evenly golden brown.

06

Drain

Remove the fried jamun and allow excess oil or ghee to drain.

07

Soak in Syrup

Place the warm jamun in the sugar syrup and allow them to absorb the sweetness.

08

Garnish & Serve

Garnish with saffron or chopped nuts and serve warm or chilled.

⏱ 45 MINUTES 🍽 6 SERVINGS 🍬 DESSERT 🇮🇳 INDIA
