RECIPE • 01

🍫 Chocolate Fudge

Rich, creamy and delicious chocolate fudge with a soft melt-in-your-mouth texture.

⏱ 25 MIN ✦ EASY
✦

🍫 Chocolate Fudge

✦ Ingredients

  • 1 cup condensed milk
  • 2 cups chocolate chips
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • ¼ cup chopped nuts
  • A pinch of salt

✦ Preparation

  1. Melt butter in a pan.
  2. Add condensed milk and chocolate chips.
  3. Stir continuously until smooth and creamy.
  4. Add vanilla essence and chopped nuts.
  5. Pour into a lined tray.
  6. Refrigerate until firm.
  7. Cut into small squares and serve.

✦ WONKA CUPCAKE
RECIPE • 02

🧁 Chocolate Cupcake

Soft chocolate cupcakes topped with creamy chocolate frosting and colourful decorations.

⏱ 35 MIN ✦ EASY
✧

🧁 Chocolate Cupcake

✦ Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • 1 tsp baking powder

✦ Preparation

  1. Mix flour, cocoa powder, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Add egg, milk, butter and vanilla.
  3. Mix until smooth.
  4. Pour batter into cupcake moulds.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 18–22 minutes.
  6. Cool completely.
  7. Decorate with chocolate frosting and sprinkles.
