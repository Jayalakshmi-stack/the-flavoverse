✦ THE THAI TABLE ✦

A Taste of Thailand

Thai cuisine is known for its beautiful balance of sweet, sour, salty and spicy flavours. From street-food favourites to creamy curries and tropical desserts, every dish brings together fresh herbs and bold aromas.

✦ CULINARY JOURNEY ✦

🍽️ Three Thai Classics

Follow every recipe from ingredients to preparation

01

🍜 Pad Thai

THAILAND'S FAMOUS STIR-FRIED NOODLES

TANGY • SAVOURY • DELICIOUS

🌿 Ingredients

Rice Noodles Tofu Egg Bean Sprouts Garlic Tamarind Paste Fish Sauce Peanuts Spring Onion Lime

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Soak the Noodles

Soak the rice noodles until they become flexible but are not overly soft.

02

Prepare the Sauce

Mix tamarind paste with fish sauce and a little sugar to create the signature sweet, sour and salty Pad Thai sauce.

03

Stir-Fry the Aromatics

Heat a pan and cook garlic until fragrant. Add tofu and cook until lightly golden.

04

Add the Egg

Push the ingredients aside and cook the egg before mixing everything together.

05

Add the Noodles

Add the softened noodles and pour in the prepared sauce. Toss everything together over moderate heat.

06

Add Fresh Ingredients

Add bean sprouts and spring onion. Toss briefly so they remain fresh and crisp.

07

Garnish

Sprinkle crushed peanuts over the noodles and add a wedge of fresh lime.

08

Serve

Serve the Pad Thai immediately while warm and aromatic.

⏱ 30 MINUTES 🍽 2 SERVINGS 🍜 MAIN DISH 🇹🇭 THAILAND
✦
02

🍛 Green Curry

CREAMY • HERBAL • AROMATIC

CREAMY • SPICY • FRAGRANT

🌿 Ingredients

Chicken Green Curry Paste Coconut Milk Thai Basil Green Chilli Eggplant Fish Sauce Palm Sugar Lime Leaves

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Heat the Coconut Milk

Warm part of the coconut milk in a pan until it becomes fragrant and slightly thickened.

02

Add Green Curry Paste

Stir the green curry paste into the coconut milk and cook until aromatic.

03

Cook the Chicken

Add chicken pieces and cook gently until they are properly cooked.

04

Add Coconut Milk

Pour in the remaining coconut milk and bring the curry to a gentle simmer.

05

Add Vegetables

Add eggplant and other vegetables. Cook until tender while keeping their natural texture.

06

Balance the Flavours

Add fish sauce and palm sugar. Adjust the balance of salty and sweet flavours.

07

Add Fresh Herbs

Add Thai basil and lime leaves near the end of cooking for fresh aroma.

08

Serve

Serve hot Thai Green Curry with steamed jasmine rice.

⏱ 40 MINUTES 🍽 4 SERVINGS 🍛 MAIN DISH 🇹🇭 THAILAND
✦
03

🥭 Mango Sticky Rice

SWEET • CREAMY • TROPICAL

TROPICAL • SWEET • CREAMY

🌿 Ingredients

Sticky Rice Ripe Mango Coconut Milk Sugar Salt Sesame Seeds

🔥 Preparation Journey

01

Soak the Sticky Rice

Rinse the sticky rice and soak it before steaming.

02

Steam the Rice

Steam the soaked rice until the grains become soft and sticky.

03

Prepare Coconut Sauce

Warm coconut milk with sugar and a small amount of salt until smooth.

04

Mix with Rice

Pour part of the warm coconut mixture over the steamed sticky rice.

05

Let the Rice Absorb

Allow the sticky rice to rest so it absorbs the creamy coconut flavour.

06

Prepare the Mango

Peel and slice ripe mangoes into attractive serving pieces.

07

Arrange the Dessert

Place coconut sticky rice alongside the fresh mango slices.

08

Garnish & Serve

Drizzle with coconut sauce and sprinkle sesame seeds before serving.

⏱ 35 MINUTES 🍽 4 SERVINGS 🥭 DESSERT 🇹🇭 THAILAND
