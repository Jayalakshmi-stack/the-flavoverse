🥞 Doraemon's Dorayaki

Soft and fluffy Japanese pancakes filled with sweet red bean paste, inspired by Doraemon's favourite treat.

⏱ 30 mins ⭐ Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence
  • Red bean paste

Preparation

  1. Beat eggs and sugar until smooth.
  2. Add honey and vanilla essence.
  3. Mix flour and baking powder into batter.
  4. Heat non-stick pan on low-medium.
  5. Pour small batter portions and cook until bubbles appear.
  6. Flip and cook the other side.
  7. Spread red bean paste between two pancakes and serve.

🍜 Doraemon's Ramen Bowl

A warm and comforting noodle bowl inspired by the delicious meals enjoyed in Doraemon's world.

⏱ 25 mins ⭐ Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 packet ramen noodles
  • 2 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 boiled egg
  • Spring onions
  • Salt to taste

Preparation

  1. Boil vegetable stock.
  2. Add soy sauce and sesame oil.
  3. Add mixed vegetables and cook until slightly soft.
  4. Add ramen noodles and cook according to the packet instructions.
  5. Season with salt if needed.
  6. Transfer to a bowl.
  7. Add boiled egg and spring onions and serve hot.
