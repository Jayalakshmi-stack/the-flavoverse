✦ MAGICAL DRINK
RECIPE • 01

🧈 Butterbeer

A creamy wizarding drink with delicious caramel and butterscotch flavours.

⏱ 10 MIN ✦ EASY
✦

🧈 Butterbeer

✦ Ingredients

  • 1 cup chilled cream soda
  • 2 tbsp caramel syrup
  • 1 tbsp butterscotch syrup
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence
  • 2 tbsp whipped cream
  • A pinch of cinnamon

✦ Preparation

  1. Pour chilled cream soda into a glass.
  2. Add caramel and butterscotch syrup.
  3. Add vanilla essence and mix gently.
  4. Top with whipped cream.
  5. Drizzle caramel syrup and sprinkle cinnamon.

✦ MAGICAL PASTRY
RECIPE • 02

🥧 Pumpkin Pasties

Golden pastry pockets filled with pumpkin, honey and warm spices.

⏱ 45 MIN ✦ MEDIUM
✧

🥧 Pumpkin Pasties

✦ Ingredients

  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • 3 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp honey

✦ Preparation

  1. Mix flour, butter and powdered sugar.
  2. Add milk and make a soft dough.
  3. Chill the dough for 15 minutes.
  4. Mix pumpkin with honey and spices.
  5. Roll dough and cut circles.
  6. Add filling and seal the edges.
  7. Bake at 180°C for 20–25 minutes.
