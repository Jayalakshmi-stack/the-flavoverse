BRAZILIAN KITCHEN • RECIPE 01

🍲 Feijoada

🌿 🍲
CLASSIC BRAZILIAN FEIJOADA
ICONIC BRAZILIAN COMFORT FOOD

Rich, Hearty & Full of Tradition

Feijoada is a famous Brazilian black bean stew traditionally enjoyed with rice, greens and other accompaniments. It is known for its rich flavour and comforting texture.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • Black beans
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomatoes
  • Bay leaves
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01Rinse the black beans and soak them before cooking.
02Drain the beans and place them in a large cooking pot.
03Add water and bay leaves and begin simmering.
04Sauté chopped onion and garlic until fragrant.
05Add the sautéed mixture to the simmering beans.
06Season with salt and black pepper.
07Continue cooking until the beans become soft and rich.
08Serve warm with rice and traditional side dishes.
⏱ 90 MINUTES🍽 4 SERVINGS 🍲 MAIN DISH🇧🇷 BRAZIL

BRAZILIAN KITCHEN • RECIPE 02

🧀 Pão de Queijo

🌿 🧀
BRAZILIAN CHEESE BREAD
BELOVED BRAZILIAN CHEESE BREAD

Warm, Cheesy & Delightfully Soft

Pão de queijo is a popular Brazilian cheese bread made with tapioca flour. These small golden bites are naturally chewy inside and perfect as a snack or breakfast treat.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • Tapioca flour
  • Cheese
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Parmesan
  • Olive oil
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01Place tapioca flour in a large mixing bowl.
02Warm milk, butter and salt together.
03Pour the warm mixture over the tapioca flour.
04Mix well and allow the mixture to cool slightly.
05Add eggs and grated cheese to the mixture.
06Mix until a soft dough forms.
07Shape small balls and place them on a baking tray.
08Bake until golden and serve warm.
⏱ 30 MINUTES🍽 6 SERVINGS 🧀 SNACK🇧🇷 BRAZIL

BRAZILIAN KITCHEN • RECIPE 03

🍫 Brigadeiro

🌿 🍫
CLASSIC BRAZILIAN BRIGADEIRO
FAMOUS BRAZILIAN CHOCOLATE SWEET

Chocolate, Creamy & Irresistible

Brigadeiro is one of Brazil's most beloved sweets. These small chocolate treats are traditionally made with condensed milk, cocoa and butter, then coated with chocolate sprinkles.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • Condensed milk
  • Cocoa powder
  • Butter
  • Chocolate
  • Chocolate sprinkles
  • Vanilla
  • Salt
  • Cream
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01Add condensed milk to a heavy saucepan.
02Add cocoa powder and butter to the saucepan.
03Mix everything together over low heat.
04Stir continuously until the mixture thickens.
05Remove from heat and allow the mixture to cool.
06Grease your hands lightly and shape small balls.
07Roll each chocolate ball in chocolate sprinkles.
08Place them in small paper cups and serve.
⏱ 25 MINUTES🍽 12 SERVINGS 🍫 DESSERT🇧🇷 BRAZIL
