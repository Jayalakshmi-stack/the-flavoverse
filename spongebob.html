🍔 Krabby Patty

A delicious homemade veggie burger packed with fresh vegetables, cheese and a creamy burger sauce.

⏱ 25 mins 🍔 Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 vegetable burger patties
  • 2 cheese slices
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion slices
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tsp mustard sauce

Preparation

  1. Mix mayonnaise, ketchup and mustard to prepare the burger sauce.
  2. Heat a pan and cook the vegetable patties until golden on both sides.
  3. Place a cheese slice on each patty and allow it to soften.
  4. Lightly toast the burger buns.
  5. Spread the prepared sauce on the buns.
  6. Add lettuce, tomato and onion slices.
  7. Place the cheesy patty on top.
  8. Cover with the top bun and serve fresh.

RECIPE • 02

🍪 SpongeBob Cookies

Cute and buttery cookies decorated with colourful icing, perfect for a cheerful cartoon-inspired treat.

⏱ 35 mins 🍪 Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • Yellow food colouring
  • White and brown icing
  • Chocolate chips

Preparation

  1. Cream the butter and powdered sugar until light and smooth.
  2. Add vanilla essence and milk.
  3. Gradually add flour and make a soft dough.
  4. Roll the dough and cut it into fun cookie shapes.
  5. Bake at 180°C for about 10–12 minutes.
  6. Allow the cookies to cool completely.
  7. Decorate with yellow, white and brown icing to create SpongeBob-style faces.
  8. Add small chocolate details and serve.
