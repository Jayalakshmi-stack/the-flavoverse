🍫 Chocobi Cookies

Crunchy chocolate cookies inspired by Shinchan's favourite Chocobi snack.

⏱ 30 mins 🍪 Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence
  • Chocolate chips

Preparation

  1. Cream butter and powdered sugar.
  2. Add flour, cocoa and vanilla.
  3. Mix into a soft dough.
  4. Shape into cookies.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 12–15 minutes.
  6. Cool and enjoy.

🍜 Shinchan's Noodle Bowl

A colourful and comforting noodle bowl perfect for a fun cartoon-inspired meal.

⏱ 20 mins 🍜 Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 packet noodles
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Spring onions

Preparation

  1. Cook noodles according to packet instructions.
  2. Sauté vegetables in oil.
  3. Add soy sauce and chilli sauce.
  4. Add cooked noodles.
  5. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Garnish with spring onions.
