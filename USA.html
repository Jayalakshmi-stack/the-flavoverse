AMERICAN KITCHEN • RECIPE 01

🍔 Burger

🌿 🍔
CLASSIC AMERICAN BURGER
ICONIC AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD

The Classic American Bite

The burger is one of America's most beloved comfort foods. A seasoned patty is placed inside a soft bun and layered with cheese, fresh vegetables and classic toppings.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • Beef patty
  • Burger buns
  • Cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Ketchup
  • Salt & pepper
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01 Season the ground beef with salt and pepper.
02 Shape the seasoned beef into an even patty.
03 Cook the patty on a heated pan or grill until properly cooked.
04 Add cheese on top and allow it to melt.
05 Slice the burger bun and lightly toast it.
06 Spread ketchup or your favourite sauce over the bun.
07 Add lettuce, tomato, onion and the cooked patty.
08 Close the burger and serve warm.
⏱ 30 MINUTES 🍽 2 SERVINGS 🍔 MAIN DISH 🇺🇸 USA

AMERICAN KITCHEN • RECIPE 02

🥞 Pancakes

🌾 🥞
FLUFFY AMERICAN PANCAKES
BELOVED AMERICAN BREAKFAST

A Fluffy Morning Favourite

American pancakes are soft, fluffy and traditionally served in a stack. They are often enjoyed with butter, maple syrup and fresh fruits.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • All-purpose flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Vanilla
  • Maple syrup
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01 Mix flour, sugar and baking powder in a bowl.
02 Whisk milk, eggs and vanilla in another bowl.
03 Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients.
04 Mix gently until a smooth batter is formed.
05 Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat.
06 Pour some batter onto the hot pan.
07 Cook until bubbles appear, then flip and cook the other side.
08 Stack the pancakes and serve with butter and maple syrup.
⏱ 20 MINUTES 🍽 4 SERVINGS 🥞 BREAKFAST 🇺🇸 USA

AMERICAN KITCHEN • RECIPE 03

🥧 Apple Pie

🍎 🌿
CLASSIC AMERICAN APPLE PIE
FAMOUS AMERICAN DESSERT

A Slice of American Tradition

Apple pie is a much-loved American dessert made with sweet apples, warm spices and a golden pastry crust. It is a comforting classic enjoyed across generations.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • Apples
  • All-purpose flour
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon
  • Nutmeg
  • Salt
  • Pastry dough
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01 Wash, peel and slice the apples into thin pieces.
02 Mix the apples with sugar, cinnamon and nutmeg.
03 Toss the apples gently until evenly coated.
04 Place the pastry into a pie dish to form the bottom crust.
05 Fill the crust with the prepared apple mixture.
06 Cover the apples with the top pastry and seal the edges.
07 Make small openings on the top crust for steam.
08 Bake until the crust is golden and the apples are tender.
⏱ 1 HOUR 🍽 6 SERVINGS 🥧 DESSERT 🇺🇸 USA
