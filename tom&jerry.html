🧀 Cheese Platter

A colourful and elegant platter with creamy cheese, fresh fruits, crunchy crackers and delicious toppings.

⏱ 15 mins 🧀 Easy

Ingredients

  • Cheese cubes or slices
  • Crackers
  • Grapes
  • Apple slices
  • Almonds or cashews
  • Olives
  • Honey or fruit jam

Preparation

  1. Cut the cheese into small cubes or attractive slices.
  2. Wash the grapes and slice the apple.
  3. Arrange the cheese in the centre of a large serving plate.
  4. Place crackers, fruits, nuts and olives around the cheese.
  5. Add a small bowl of honey or jam.
  6. Serve fresh and enjoy.

RECIPE • 02

🥛 Milk & Cookies

Warm homemade chocolate chip cookies served with a refreshing glass of milk for the perfect classic combination.

⏱ 30 mins 🍪 Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup white sugar
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence
  • ½ cup chocolate chips
  • 1 glass chilled or warm milk

Preparation

  1. Cream the butter and both sugars until smooth.
  2. Add vanilla essence and mix well.
  3. Add flour and baking powder gradually.
  4. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  5. Shape the dough into small balls and place them on a baking tray.
  6. Bake at 180°C for about 10–12 minutes.
  7. Allow the cookies to cool slightly and serve with milk.
