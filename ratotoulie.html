RECIPE • 01

🍆 Classic Ratatouille

A colourful French vegetable dish layered with eggplant, zucchini, peppers and tomatoes with fragrant herbs.

⏱ 60 MIN ✦ MEDIUM
✦

🍆 Classic Ratatouille

✦ Ingredients

  • 1 medium eggplant, thinly sliced
  • 1 zucchini, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 red or yellow bell pepper
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup tomato puree
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper and mixed herbs
  • Fresh basil to garnish

✦ Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cook the onion and garlic in olive oil until soft.
  3. Add tomato puree, salt, pepper and herbs; simmer briefly.
  4. Spread the sauce across the bottom of a baking dish.
  5. Arrange the sliced vegetables in alternating layers.
  6. Drizzle with a little olive oil and season lightly.
  7. Cover and bake until the vegetables are tender, then uncover briefly.
  8. Garnish with fresh basil and serve warm.

✦ FRENCH DESSERT
RECIPE • 02

🍮 Crème Brûlée

A silky vanilla custard finished with a delicate, crisp caramelized sugar topping.

⏱ 50 MIN + CHILL ✦ MEDIUM
✧

🍮 Crème Brûlée

✦ Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Extra sugar for the topping

✦ Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 150°C.
  2. Warm the cream gently in a saucepan; do not boil.
  3. Whisk the egg yolks and sugar until combined.
  4. Slowly whisk the warm cream into the egg mixture.
  5. Add vanilla, then pour into oven-safe ramekins.
  6. Place ramekins in a baking dish and add hot water around them.
  7. Bake until the custard is just set, then cool.
  8. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
  9. Sprinkle a thin layer of sugar on top and caramelize carefully with suitable kitchen equipment.
