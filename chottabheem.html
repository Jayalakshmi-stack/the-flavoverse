🟠 SWEET
RECIPE • 01

🟠 Chhota Bheem's Laddoo

Soft and delicious besan laddoos made with roasted gram flour, ghee and sugar, inspired by Bheem's love for laddoos.

⏱ 30 mins 🟠 Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • ¼ cup ghee
  • 2 tbsp chopped cashews
  • 1 tbsp raisins
  • ½ tsp cardamom powder

Preparation

  1. Heat ghee in a thick-bottomed pan.
  2. Add besan and roast it on low heat, stirring continuously.
  3. Continue roasting until the besan becomes fragrant and golden.
  4. Add chopped cashews, raisins and cardamom powder.
  5. Allow the mixture to cool slightly.
  6. Add powdered sugar and mix well.
  7. Take small portions and shape them into round laddoos.
  8. Allow them to cool completely before serving.

🥛 DRINK
RECIPE • 02

🥛 Dholakpur Badam Milk

A creamy and aromatic almond milk flavoured with cardamom and saffron, perfect for a comforting drink.

⏱ 20 mins 🥛 Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 10–12 almonds
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2–3 saffron strands
  • ¼ tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tbsp chopped pistachios

Preparation

  1. Soak the almonds in warm water for about 15 minutes.
  2. Peel the almonds and grind them into a smooth paste.
  3. Heat the milk in a saucepan.
  4. Add the almond paste and stir well.
  5. Add sugar, saffron and cardamom powder.
  6. Simmer on low heat for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Garnish with chopped pistachios.
  8. Serve warm or chilled.
