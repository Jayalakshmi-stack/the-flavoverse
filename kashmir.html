Welcome to Kashmir, a land famous for its breathtaking valleys, rich culture, traditional hospitality, and aromatic cuisine. 🏔️ About Kashmir Cuisine

Kashmiri cuisine is one of the most distinctive culinary traditions of India. It combines aromatic spices, saffron, yogurt, dry fruits, rice, and carefully selected herbs to create dishes with rich but balanced flavours.

Kashmiri cooking is especially famous for its traditional Wazwan culture, where a variety of elaborate dishes are prepared and served during weddings, festivals, and special celebrations. Meat-based dishes are an important part of this culinary tradition.

Popular dishes such as Rogan Josh and Yakhni showcase the delicate use of spices and yogurt, while Kashmiri Pulao and Modur Pulao highlight the region's love for fragrant rice, dry fruits, and sweet aromatic flavours.

The cuisine of Kashmir beautifully represents the region's culture, hospitality, history, and connection with local ingredients. Every recipe carries a unique combination of aroma, tradition, and royal culinary heritage.

🍛 Rogan Josh

Rogan Josh is one of the most famous dishes of Kashmiri cuisine. It is a rich and aromatic curry traditionally prepared with tender meat, yogurt, Kashmiri chilli, and fragrant whole spices. The dish is known for its beautiful red colour, deep aroma, and balanced flavour. Rogan Josh is an important part of traditional Kashmiri feasts and is often enjoyed with steamed rice or traditional breads.

🥘 Ingredients

  • 500 g Mutton
  • 1 cup Yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 Cinnamon Stick
  • 4 Green Cardamom Pods
  • 3 Cloves
  • 1 Bay Leaf
  • 2 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • Salt to Taste

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Wash the mutton pieces and drain the excess water.
  2. Heat mustard oil in a heavy-bottomed pan.
  3. Add cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and bay leaf.
  4. Add the mutton pieces and cook until lightly browned.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell disappears.
  6. Mix Kashmiri chilli powder and turmeric powder with a little water.
  7. Add the spice mixture to the mutton and cook well.
  8. Whisk the yogurt and slowly add it while stirring continuously.
  9. Add enough water, cover the pan, and cook until the meat becomes tender.
  10. Add garam masala and adjust the salt.
  11. Cook for a few more minutes until the gravy becomes aromatic.
  12. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.

⏱ Cooking Time

90 Minutes

🍽 Serves

4 People

⭐ Difficulty

Medium

👨‍🍳 Chef's Tip

Use good-quality Kashmiri chilli powder for the beautiful colour and aroma. Add yogurt slowly while stirring to keep the gravy smooth.

🌟 Fun Fact

Rogan Josh is one of the signature dishes associated with Kashmiri cuisine and is traditionally served as part of elaborate Kashmiri feasts.

🥣 Yakhni

Yakhni is a traditional Kashmiri preparation known for its delicate yogurt-based gravy and aromatic whole spices. It is usually prepared with tender meat, yogurt, fennel, ginger, cardamom, and other spices. Unlike heavily spiced curries, Yakhni has a mild and soothing flavour that allows the aroma of the spices and yogurt to stand out.

🥘 Ingredients

  • 500 g Mutton
  • 1½ cups Yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Fennel Powder
  • 1 tsp Dry Ginger Powder
  • 1 Cinnamon Stick
  • 4 Green Cardamom Pods
  • 3 Cloves
  • 1 Bay Leaf
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 2 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • Salt to Taste

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Wash the mutton pieces and keep them aside.
  2. Heat mustard oil in a heavy pan.
  3. Add cumin seeds, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and bay leaf.
  4. Add the mutton and lightly brown the pieces.
  5. Add enough water and cook until the meat becomes tender.
  6. Whisk the yogurt until smooth.
  7. Add fennel powder and dry ginger powder to the yogurt.
  8. Slowly add the yogurt mixture to the cooked mutton while stirring.
  9. Cook on low flame until the gravy becomes smooth and aromatic.
  10. Adjust salt and simmer for a few minutes.
  11. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.

⏱ Cooking Time

70 Minutes

🍽 Serves

4 People

⭐ Difficulty

Medium

👨‍🍳 Chef's Tip

Always add yogurt slowly over low heat while stirring continuously. This helps maintain a smooth and creamy gravy.

🌟 Fun Fact

Yakhni is loved for its mild flavour and aromatic gravy and is one of the well-known traditional preparations of Kashmiri cuisine.

🍚 Kashmiri Pulao

Kashmiri Pulao is a fragrant rice preparation famous for its beautiful combination of aromatic rice, saffron, dry fruits, nuts, and fresh fruits. It is lighter and sweeter than many spicy rice dishes and is often served during celebrations and special occasions.

🥘 Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice
  • 3 cups Water
  • 1 Onion (Thinly Sliced)
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 Cinnamon Stick
  • 3 Cardamom Pods
  • 3 Cloves
  • A Few Saffron Strands
  • ¼ cup Cashew Nuts
  • ¼ cup Almonds
  • 2 tbsp Raisins
  • ½ cup Apple Pieces
  • ½ cup Pomegranate Seeds
  • Fresh Mint Leaves
  • Salt to Taste

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 20 minutes.
  2. Heat ghee in a heavy pan.
  3. Add cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves.
  4. Add sliced onions and cook until golden brown.
  5. Add the soaked rice and gently mix.
  6. Add water and salt and cook until the rice is almost done.
  7. Add saffron soaked in warm water.
  8. Cover and cook on low flame until the rice becomes fluffy.
  9. Roast cashews, almonds, and raisins separately in a little ghee.
  10. Gently mix the roasted nuts with the rice.
  11. Garnish with apple pieces, pomegranate seeds, and mint leaves.
  12. Serve hot.

⏱ Cooking Time

40 Minutes

🍽 Serves

4 People

⭐ Difficulty

Easy

👨‍🍳 Chef's Tip

Use long-grain basmati rice and soak it before cooking. Add saffron near the end to preserve its aroma and beautiful colour.

🌟 Fun Fact

Kashmiri Pulao is especially known for its colourful presentation, with dry fruits, nuts, fruits, and saffron giving the dish a festive appearance.

🍚 Modur Pulao

Modur Pulao is a traditional sweet Kashmiri rice preparation. The word "Modur" refers to sweetness, and this dish combines fragrant basmati rice with saffron, sugar, dry fruits, nuts, and aromatic spices. It is often prepared for festivals, celebrations, and special occasions.

🥘 Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice
  • 3 cups Water
  • ½ cup Sugar
  • 3 tbsp Ghee
  • A Few Saffron Strands
  • 1 Cinnamon Stick
  • 3 Cardamom Pods
  • 3 Cloves
  • ¼ cup Cashew Nuts
  • ¼ cup Almonds
  • 2 tbsp Raisins
  • 2 tbsp Desiccated Coconut
  • 1 tbsp Rose Water
  • Salt – A Small Pinch

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for about 20 minutes.
  2. Cook the rice until it is about 80% done and drain the excess water.
  3. Heat ghee in a heavy pan.
  4. Add cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves.
  5. Add the partially cooked rice.
  6. Add sugar and gently mix everything together.
  7. Add saffron soaked in warm water.
  8. Cover and cook on low flame until the rice becomes completely tender.
  9. Roast cashews, almonds, and raisins in a little ghee.
  10. Add the roasted nuts and raisins to the pulao.
  11. Add rose water and gently mix.
  12. Serve warm.

⏱ Cooking Time

40 Minutes

🍽 Serves

4 People

⭐ Difficulty

Easy

👨‍🍳 Chef's Tip

Cook the rice gently on low flame after adding sugar. This helps the grains remain separate while absorbing the sweet aroma.

🌟 Fun Fact

Modur Pulao is a unique Kashmiri rice dish because it combines sweetness, saffron, nuts, and aromatic spices in one preparation.

🏔️ Kashmir Food Facts

Kashmiri saffron is highly valued for its aroma, colour, and flavour and is used in several traditional dishes.

Almonds, walnuts, cashews, raisins, and other dry fruits are commonly used in Kashmiri dishes.

🍲 Wazwan Tradition

Wazwan is an important part of Kashmiri culinary culture, especially during weddings and special celebrations.

🌿 Aromatic Spices

Fennel, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, and dry ginger are important ingredients in traditional Kashmiri cooking.
