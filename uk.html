BRITISH KITCHEN • RECIPE 01

🐟 Fish & Chips

🌿 🐟
CLASSIC BRITISH FISH & CHIPS
ICONIC BRITISH COMFORT FOOD

The Classic Seaside Favourite

Fish and chips is one of Britain's most famous comfort foods. Crispy battered fish is served with golden chips and is often enjoyed with lemon, peas or traditional sauces.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • White fish fillets
  • Potatoes
  • All-purpose flour
  • Baking powder
  • Cold water
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Lemon
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01Wash and cut the potatoes into thick chips.
02Soak the potato chips in cold water and drain them well.
03Pat the fish fillets dry and season lightly with salt.
04Mix flour, baking powder and cold water to make a smooth batter.
05Coat the fish fillets evenly with the prepared batter.
06Cook the chips until golden and crisp.
07Cook the battered fish until crisp and fully cooked.
08Serve the fish and chips warm with lemon and your favourite sauce.
⏱ 40 MINUTES 🍽 2 SERVINGS 🐟 MAIN DISH 🇬🇧 UK

BRITISH KITCHEN • RECIPE 02

🥧 Shepherd's Pie

🌿 🥧
HEARTY SHEPHERD'S PIE
TRADITIONAL BRITISH FAMILY MEAL

A Warm Homestyle Classic

Shepherd's pie is a comforting British dish made with seasoned minced lamb and vegetables, covered with creamy mashed potatoes and baked until the top becomes beautifully golden.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • Minced lamb
  • Potatoes
  • Onion
  • Carrots
  • Peas
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Herbs & seasoning
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01Peel and boil the potatoes until they become tender.
02Mash the potatoes with butter and a little milk.
03Heat a pan and cook the chopped onions until soft.
04Add minced lamb and cook until properly browned.
05Add carrots, peas, herbs and seasoning.
06Cook the filling until the vegetables become tender.
07Transfer the filling to a baking dish and spread mashed potatoes on top.
08Bake until the potato topping becomes golden and serve warm.
⏱ 1 HOUR 🍽 4 SERVINGS 🥧 MAIN DISH 🇬🇧 UK

BRITISH KITCHEN • RECIPE 03

🍮 Sticky Toffee Pudding

🌿 🍮
CLASSIC BRITISH STICKY TOFFEE PUDDING
BELOVED BRITISH DESSERT

A Rich and Comforting Sweet

Sticky toffee pudding is a classic British dessert made with soft date sponge and covered with a warm toffee sauce. It is traditionally served with cream or ice cream.

🥣 MAIN INGREDIENTS
  • Dates
  • All-purpose flour
  • Butter
  • Brown sugar
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla
  • Baking soda
  • Toffee sauce
🔨 THE 8-STEP JOURNEY
01Chop the dates and soak them in hot water until soft.
02Mix flour and baking soda together in a bowl.
03Beat butter and brown sugar until smooth and creamy.
04Add eggs and vanilla and mix gently.
05Add the flour mixture and softened dates to form the batter.
06Pour the batter into a prepared baking dish.
07Bake until the sponge is soft, risen and cooked through.
08Pour warm toffee sauce over the pudding and serve.
⏱ 50 MINUTES 🍽 6 SERVINGS 🍮 DESSERT 🇬🇧 UK
