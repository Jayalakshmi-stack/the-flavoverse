🕌 About Uttar Pradesh Cuisine

Uttar Pradesh is one of India's richest culinary destinations, renowned for its royal Awadhi cuisine, Mughlai influences, and centuries-old food traditions. The state is especially famous for the city of Lucknow, where skilled royal chefs perfected the art of slow cooking, creating dishes with delicate flavors, aromatic spices, and elegant presentation.

The cuisine of Uttar Pradesh offers a delightful variety of kebabs, biryanis, rich curries, breads, and traditional desserts. Popular dishes such as Galouti Kebab, Lucknowi Biryani, Kakori Kebab, and Shahi Tukda beautifully represent the state's royal heritage. These recipes continue to be enjoyed during festivals, family celebrations, weddings, and special occasions.

Traditional cooking methods such as "Dum Pukht" (slow cooking in sealed pots) are widely used to enhance aroma and flavor. Fresh herbs, saffron, yogurt, dry fruits, and fragrant spices play an important role in creating the rich taste that makes Awadhi cuisine famous around the world.

Whether you enjoy the melt-in-the-mouth Galouti Kebab, the aromatic Lucknowi Biryani, the delicious Kakori Kebab, or the sweet richness of Shahi Tukda, every dish reflects the royal hospitality, culture, and timeless culinary excellence of Uttar Pradesh.

🥩 Galouti Kebab

Galouti Kebab is one of the most famous royal delicacies of Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Created during the Nawabi era, these kebabs are known for their incredibly soft texture that almost melts in the mouth. They are prepared using finely minced meat, aromatic spices, herbs, and cooked on a hot griddle with ghee. Galouti Kebab is traditionally served with Roomali Roti, Paratha, mint chutney, and sliced onions.

🥘 Ingredients

  • 500 g Minced Mutton
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 1 Onion (Finely Chopped)
  • 2 Green Chilies (Finely Chopped)
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • ½ tsp Cardamom Powder
  • 1 tbsp Roasted Gram Flour
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • Salt to Taste

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Mix minced mutton with ginger-garlic paste and all the spices.
  2. Add chopped onions, green chilies, coriander leaves, and roasted gram flour.
  3. Mix everything thoroughly until smooth.
  4. Cover and marinate the mixture for at least 1 hour.
  5. Shape the mixture into small round patties.
  6. Heat ghee on a flat pan or tawa.
  7. Cook the kebabs on medium heat until golden brown on both sides.
  8. Serve hot with Roomali Roti, mint chutney, and onion rings.

⏱ Cooking Time

45 Minutes

🍽 Serves

4 People

⭐ Difficulty

Medium

👨‍🍳 Chef's Tip

Marinate the meat well and cook the kebabs on low to medium heat with ghee. This helps them remain soft, juicy, and full of authentic Awadhi flavor.

🌟 Fun Fact

Galouti Kebab was created for an elderly Nawab of Lucknow who had lost his teeth but still wanted to enjoy delicious kebabs. Its soft, melt-in-the-mouth texture made it a royal favorite.

🍛 Lucknowi Biryani

Lucknowi Biryani, also known as Awadhi Biryani, is one of the most famous royal dishes of Uttar Pradesh. Prepared using fragrant basmati rice, tender marinated meat, aromatic spices, saffron, and herbs, this biryani is slow-cooked using the traditional Dum Pukht method. Unlike spicy biryanis, Lucknowi Biryani is known for its mild yet rich flavors and irresistible aroma. It is a favorite dish served during festivals, weddings, and royal feasts.

🥘 Ingredients

  • 500 g Chicken or Mutton
  • 2 cups Basmati Rice
  • 2 Onions (Thinly Sliced)
  • 1 cup Yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 2 Green Chilies
  • Whole Spices (Bay Leaf, Cinnamon, Cardamom, Cloves)
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • Saffron soaked in Warm Milk
  • Fresh Mint Leaves
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • 3 tbsp Ghee
  • Salt to Taste

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Marinate the chicken or mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, and salt for at least one hour.
  2. Cook the basmati rice until it is about 70% done.
  3. Heat ghee and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  4. Cook the marinated meat until it becomes tender.
  5. Layer the partially cooked rice over the cooked meat.
  6. Top with fried onions, mint leaves, coriander leaves, and saffron milk.
  7. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low flame (Dum) for 20–25 minutes.
  8. Gently mix before serving.
  9. Serve hot with raita and salad.

⏱ Cooking Time

90 Minutes

🍽 Serves

4–5 People

⭐ Difficulty

Medium

👨‍🍳 Chef's Tip

For authentic Awadhi flavor, always use long-grain basmati rice, pure ghee, saffron, and cook the biryani on low heat using the Dum method. This locks in the aroma and makes every grain flavorful.

🌟 Fun Fact

Lucknowi Biryani originated in the royal kitchens of the Nawabs of Awadh. It is famous for its delicate flavors and elegant cooking style, making it one of India's most celebrated biryanis.

🍞 Shahi Tukda

Shahi Tukda is one of the most famous royal desserts of Uttar Pradesh, especially associated with the royal kitchens of Lucknow. This rich delicacy is prepared by frying slices of bread in ghee until golden brown and soaking them in fragrant sugar syrup. It is then topped with creamy rabri, saffron, cardamom, almonds, pistachios, and silver leaf for a luxurious finish. Shahi Tukda is a popular dessert served during festivals, weddings, and special celebrations.

🥘 Ingredients

  • 6 Bread Slices
  • 3 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 cup Sugar
  • ½ cup Water
  • 1 cup Rabri
  • ½ tsp Cardamom Powder
  • A few Saffron Strands
  • 2 tbsp Chopped Almonds
  • 2 tbsp Chopped Pistachios
  • Silver Leaf (Optional)

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Trim the edges of the bread and cut each slice into triangles.
  2. Heat ghee and fry the bread slices until golden brown and crispy.
  3. Prepare a sugar syrup by boiling sugar and water together.
  4. Dip each fried bread slice briefly into the warm sugar syrup.
  5. Arrange the bread pieces on a serving plate.
  6. Pour chilled rabri generously over the bread.
  7. Garnish with saffron, cardamom powder, almonds, pistachios, and silver leaf.
  8. Serve chilled or slightly warm.

⏱ Cooking Time

35 Minutes

🍽 Serves

4 People

⭐ Difficulty

Easy

👨‍🍳 Chef's Tip

Use fresh homemade rabri and serve the dessert chilled. Garnishing with saffron and dry fruits gives Shahi Tukda its authentic royal taste and elegant appearance.

🌟 Fun Fact

The name "Shahi Tukda" means "Royal Piece." This dessert was a favorite of the Nawabs of Awadh and continues to be one of the most popular Mughlai sweets in North India.

🍢 Kakori Kebab

Kakori Kebab is one of the most famous royal delicacies of Lucknow in Uttar Pradesh. Originating from the town of Kakori, these soft and juicy kebabs are prepared using finely minced mutton blended with aromatic spices and herbs. Traditionally cooked over charcoal, Kakori Kebabs are known for their silky texture and rich flavor. They are commonly served with Roomali Roti, mint chutney, onion rings, and lemon wedges during festivals, family gatherings, and royal feasts.

🥘 Ingredients

  • 500 g Minced Mutton
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 1 Onion (Finely Chopped)
  • 2 Green Chilies (Finely Chopped)
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • ½ tsp Cardamom Powder
  • 1 tbsp Roasted Gram Flour
  • Fresh Coriander Leaves
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • Salt to Taste
  • Wooden Skewers

👩‍🍳 Preparation

  1. Combine minced mutton with ginger-garlic paste, spices, herbs, and roasted gram flour.
  2. Mix thoroughly until the mixture becomes smooth and well combined.
  3. Cover and marinate for 2 hours to enhance the flavor.
  4. Shape the mixture evenly around soaked wooden skewers.
  5. Heat a grill pan or charcoal grill and brush it with ghee.
  6. Cook the kebabs on medium heat, turning gently until evenly browned.
  7. Brush with a little extra ghee while cooking for added richness.
  8. Serve hot with Roomali Roti, mint chutney, onion rings, and lemon wedges.

⏱ Cooking Time

50 Minutes

🍽 Serves

4 People

⭐ Difficulty

Medium

👨‍🍳 Chef's Tip

Marinate the meat for at least two hours and cook on medium heat to keep the kebabs soft, juicy, and full of authentic Awadhi flavor.

🌟 Fun Fact

Kakori Kebab originated in the town of Kakori near Lucknow and became famous during the Nawabi era. Its tender texture and delicate spice blend have made it one of India's most celebrated kebab varieties.

🕌 Uttar Pradesh Food Facts

👑 Royal Awadhi Cuisine

Uttar Pradesh is famous for its royal Awadhi cuisine, developed in the kitchens of the Nawabs of Lucknow. The recipes are known for their rich aroma, delicate spices, and elegant presentation.

🥩 Famous Kebabs

Galouti Kebab and Kakori Kebab are among the world's most famous kebabs. Their soft texture and unique spice blend make them a signature of Lucknow's royal cuisine.

🍚 Aromatic Biryani

Lucknowi Biryani is prepared with fragrant basmati rice, saffron, herbs, and tender meat. It is known for its mild yet rich flavor and royal cooking style.

🍞 Traditional Royal Dessert

Shahi Tukda is one of Uttar Pradesh's most celebrated desserts. Prepared with fried bread, sugar syrup, and creamy rabri, it is served during festivals and weddings.

❤️ Hospitality & Heritage

The cuisine of Uttar Pradesh reflects centuries of Mughal and Awadhi traditions. Every meal is prepared with care, generosity, and warm hospitality, making it a memorable culinary experience.
