Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful answers for our food-loving community.

Can I suggest a recipe for the website?
Absolutely! Select "Recipe Suggestion" in the contact form and tell us about the dish you would love to see featured on The Flavorverse.
How can I report a website issue?
Select "Technical Support" and explain the issue you encountered. Please include the page name and the steps needed to reproduce the problem. Never include passwords or other confidential information.
Can I share feedback about a recipe?
Yes! Choose "Website Feedback" and share your suggestions. Your feedback can help us improve the recipes and the overall browsing experience.
Can I contact you about ingredient kit orders?
Yes. Choose "Ingredient Kit Order" and include your order reference if you have one. Do not include payment card details or passwords.
