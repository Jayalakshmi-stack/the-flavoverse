✦ TROPICAL SPECIAL
RECIPE • 01

🍉 Tropical Fruit Bowl

A refreshing mix of juicy tropical fruits with bright colours and natural sweetness.

⏱ 10 MIN ✦ EASY
✦

🍉 Tropical Fruit Bowl

✦ Ingredients

  • 1 cup watermelon
  • 1 banana
  • 1 cup pineapple
  • ½ cup papaya
  • ½ cup mango
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Fresh mint leaves

✦ Preparation

  1. Wash and cut all fruits into small pieces.
  2. Add watermelon, pineapple and papaya.
  3. Add mango and banana.
  4. Drizzle honey over the fruits.
  5. Mix gently.
  6. Garnish with fresh mint leaves.
  7. Serve chilled.

✦ SAVANNA BREAKFAST
RECIPE • 02

🍌 Banana Pancakes

Soft and fluffy banana pancakes with a naturally sweet tropical flavour.

⏱ 25 MIN ✦ EASY
✧

🍌 Banana Pancakes

✦ Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • ¾ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • 1 tbsp butter

✦ Preparation

  1. Mash the bananas in a bowl.
  2. Add egg and milk.
  3. Add flour, sugar and baking powder.
  4. Add vanilla essence and mix gently.
  5. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
  6. Pour batter and cook both sides until golden.
  7. Serve warm with banana slices.
